ENGINEERING ARCHITECTURE

How ChurnSense Operates

A resilient three-tier machine learning pipeline built to eliminate black-box risk and training-serving skew.

01

Unified Scikit-Learn Pipeline

Raw tabular attributes flow through a synchronized ColumnTransformer with standard numeric scaling and categorical one-hot encoding, feeding directly into a regularized classifier.

02

Mathematical SHAP Attribution

Every scoring event generates real-time Shapley values, calculating the exact positive and negative marginal contribution of each customer parameter to eliminate arbitrary predictions.

03

Generative AI Grounding

Google's Gemini Flash engine converts SHAP attributions into clear, executive 2-sentence retention rationale, guarded with strict sub-second rule-based fallbacks.
